Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Support Privacy Policy

The short version

GameVerse has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage data to deliver those ads, as described below.

What stays on your device

GameVerse saves these in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Your game progress, levels and high scores
  • Your stars, coins and unlocked content
  • Your settings, such as music and sound on or off

We do not receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Advertising

GameVerse shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • Device information, such as the device model, operating system version and language
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Information about the ads you see and tap
  • Diagnostic information about how ads perform in the app

GameVerse does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so on iPhone and iPad it does not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Google handles this data under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Photos

You can choose a picture from your photo library to use inside a game. This only happens when you choose to. The picture stays on your device and is not uploaded or shared. GameVerse does not read your photo library for any other purpose.

If you choose to save a wallpaper, GameVerse asks for permission to add it to your photo library.

What GameVerse does not do

  • No accounts, sign-up or login
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own
  • No in-app purchases
  • No selling of personal information

Children

GameVerse is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
