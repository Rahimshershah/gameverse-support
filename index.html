Common questions

How do I play with friends?

Open All Games and pick a game from Play together. Most of them let up to four people share one phone or tablet. Choose the number of players on the game's setup screen. In many games, computer players can fill the empty seats.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your progress, stars and coins are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too, and they do not move between devices.

Why do I see ads?

GameVerse is free, and ads help keep it that way. Ads are shown by Google AdMob. See the Privacy Policy for what that involves.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open the Profile tab and switch off Music or Sound Effects.
